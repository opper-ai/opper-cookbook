Live Translate

Translate live speech in real time — from your microphone, or from any browser tab (a video, a call). You hear only the translation; the source language is detected automatically.

You'll be asked to pick a window to share — choose the tab playing your audio and tick "Also share tab audio". The tab's original sound is muted locally, so you hear only the translation.

The browser connects straight to Opper over WebSocket, authenticated with a single-use ephemeral ticket. The model and target language are locked onto the ticket at mint time — your API key never reaches the browser.
